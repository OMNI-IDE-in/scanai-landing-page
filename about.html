TECHNOLOGY • MISSION

Autonomous Surgical AI & Hospital Infrastructure

ScanAI is an applied artificial intelligence and medical robotics engineering firm dedicated to eliminating procedural friction, radiation overexposure, and administrative delays in clinical care.

Core Conviction

Deterministic Autonomy Where Millimeters & Seconds Matter

Interventional surgery and hospital operations both suffer from unneeded friction: surgeons are blinded by contrast washouts and radiation burdens, while hospital teams drown in paper claims and disconnected software.

ScanAI focuses on two transformative platforms. With Omni Rovis, we engineer real-time closed-loop computer vision and adaptive visual servoing to stabilize C-arm fluoroscopy and eliminate up to 78% of scatter radiation. With Arogya Omni, we build India's premier hospital operating system certified across all ABDM milestones and powered by an automated National Health Claims Exchange engine.
